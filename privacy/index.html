Privacy Policy — Sweethearts: Date Quiz

Effective date: October 1, 2026 Publisher: Vi Truong Le Contact: tremainejerde8549@icloud.com App: Sweethearts: Date Quiz (com.vytruongle.sweethearts)

Overview

Sweethearts is a two-player quiz game for couples, played across two separate phones and connected by a one-time room code. This policy describes exactly what the app does and does not do with your information. It is written to match the app's actual behavior — not a generic template — based on the planned iOS build (blueprint/IOS_PLAN.md), which uses only Firebase Realtime Database and Firebase Anonymous Authentication. It does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution services.

What we collect

  • Display name. When you first open the app, you're asked for a name to show your partner during a game (e.g. "Minh" or "Lan"). This is stored on your device and is included in the game session data sent to Firebase so your partner's phone can show it. It is not a real-name requirement — you can use any name you like, and you can change it at any time in Settings.
  • In-game answers. The answers you pick during a show (Host's 15 personal answers, Player's 15 guesses) are sent to Firebase Realtime Database so the two phones in that session can stay in sync in real time.
  • An anonymous session identifier. The app signs in "anonymously" to Firebase on first launch, silently, with no sign-up screen. This creates a random identifier used only to control who is allowed to write to which part of a game session (e.g., only the Host can set the questions; only the Player can submit answers). It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identity, and there is no password.

We do not collect: your email address, your real name (unless you choose to type it as your display name), your phone number, your location, your contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or any financial or health information. The app does not ask for any of these.

Who can see your game data

Only the two phones taking part in one specific game session — the Host and the Player who joined with that session's 6-digit room code. There is no public profile, no public feed, and no way for anyone else to browse game sessions; a session can only be read by a device that has that session's code, and a code stops allowing new joins the moment the show has started or ended.

How your data is transmitted and stored

  • All data sent to Firebase travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Firebase Realtime Database is operated by Google; see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's data processing terms for how Google handles data it processes on our behalf.
  • A finished or abandoned game session's summary (your score, the questions, both players' answers) is also saved on your own device as your session history, so you can look back at past date nights. This local history is not automatically deleted from Firebase's servers or synced elsewhere beyond the original two participating devices.

Your choices

  • No account to delete. Because the app has no account system (no email/password, no sign-up), there is no account-deletion flow to offer — there is no account to delete.
  • Clear your local history. You can delete your on-device game history at any time from Settings ▸ Delete History. This removes it from your device; it does not retroactively remove the record from your former game partner's device, since that copy is theirs.
  • Change your display name at any time in Settings.
  • Stop using the app at any time by deleting it — this removes all on-device data immediately.

Children's privacy

Sweethearts is intended for couples and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region). If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us at the email above and we will address it.

Third-party services

The only third-party service used is Firebase (Google), for the real-time database and anonymous authentication described above. The app does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or attribution/tracking SDK. (If a future version adds any of these, this policy will be updated first, and the App Privacy label in App Store Connect will be updated to match before that version ships.)

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, note the change in the app's "What's New" release notes.
